ARM-ALPHA

AI-powered assistive technology

PythonMachine learningAudio classificationRaspberry Pi

ARM-ALPHA explores how environmental sound recognition can give people with hearing impairments more awareness of their surroundings. It pairs machine-learning experiments with an assistive hardware concept that communicates detected sounds through vibration patterns.

Microphonecaptures ambient sound
Raspberry Piruns the model
Classifiertrained on UrbanSound8K
Vibration motorssignal the detected sound
  • Built an audio-classification workflow with Python and machine-learning techniques.
  • Worked with the UrbanSound8K environmental sound dataset.
  • Connected the software experiments to a hardware concept with a Raspberry Pi, microphone and vibration motors.
  • Contributed to model development and refinement, with about 95% accuracy reported in project experiments.
The challenge is not only to classify sound, but to turn model output into useful feedback in an assistive experience.
